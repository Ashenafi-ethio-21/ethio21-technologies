The 4 Pillars of the Zero-Cost Architecture
Tier 1 • Edge Client
Client PWA & Service Worker
Runs directly in parents' and tutors' web browsers. Pre-caches all assets (HTML, CSS, JS, fonts) with offline support.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages CDN
  • Monthly Cost: $0.00 (Unlimited)
  • Asset Caching: Service Worker v128
  • Storage Impact: 0 MB Cloud Storage
Tier 2 • Hot Desk
Cloud Firestore Hot-Desk
Stores only active, pending registrations awaiting admin review and match making. Kept ultra-lean via Zero-Base64 stripping.
  • Free Quota: 1,000 MB Storage
  • Record Weight: ~0.8 KB - 1.2 KB
  • Daily Limit: 50K Reads / 20K Writes
  • Daily Reset: Every 24h at 00:00 UTC
Tier 3 • Media Vault
Telegram Supergroup Media Vault
Receives and stores high-resolution photos: Kebele IDs, Student IDs, University Diplomas, and Grade Reports via Bot API.

Tier 4 • Cold Vault
Google Sheets Executive Ledger
Permanent cold repository for matched, completed, or closed contracts. Connected via Google Apps Script serverless webhook.

Founder & Coordinator Quick Operational Rules

1. Safe National Promotion

You can share and promote Teme-Astegni nationally on TikTok, Telegram, Facebook, and Instagram with 100% confidence. Even if 1,000 parents register in a single day, writes remain under 5% of the free quota.

2. Use "Vault & Free Space"

3. Zero Unexpected Charges

Google Firebase Spark plan is a "hard-capped" plan. It cannot bill your bank account or credit card. If a free limit is ever reached, it pauses until midnight (00:00 UTC) when it automatically resets to 0.
