Section 03

3. Team Roles & Daily Responsibilities

👑 Role 1: Founder / Super Admin (Owner)
teme-admin.html • Super Admin Access
📌 Primary Responsibilities:
  • Create and manage Coordinator Admin accounts in the "Admins" tab.
  • Configure and monitor Google Sheets webhooks and Telegram channels in the "Database" tab.
  • Set overall tuition price guidelines and oversee monthly business revenue.
  • Intervene in complex disputes between parents and tutors.
📋 Role 2: Verification Admin (KYC Coordinator)
teme-admin.html > Tutors Tab & Telegram Tutor Hub
🎯 Daily Goal: Ensure every single tutor sent to a family's home is 100% verified and legitimate.
☑️ Daily Checklist:
  • Open the "Tutors" tab in teme-admin.html.
  • Filter by status: Pending Verification.
  • Review the tutor's credentials:
    • National Fayda ID / Student ID: Confirm validity and photo match.
    • University & Year: Ensure they are 2nd-year or above in a recognized university.
    • Location & Subjects: Note their neighborhood in Addis Ababa and teaching subjects.
  • If genuine, click "✅ Verify Tutor" (their status updates across Firebase and their card turns green).
  • If questionable or incomplete, contact the tutor or reject with feedback.
🤝 Role 3: Matchmaker Admin (Operations Coordinator)
teme-admin.html > Parents & Matches Tabs
🎯 Daily Goal: Connect every parent request with the best qualified tutor within 2 to 24 hours.
☑️ Daily Checklist:
  • Open the "Parents" tab to view new requests (Grade level, location, schedule, subject needs).
  • Call the parent to confirm details (e.g. specific days per week, hourly budget).
  • Go to the "Matches" tab:
    • Select the Parent request.
    • Filter verified tutors by same sub-city/neighborhood and matching subject skill.
    • Ensure the tutor is not over capacity (max 3 students per tutor).
  • Click "🔗 Create Match".
  • The match record is instantly generated, published to Telegram, and updated in both the parent's and tutor's app screens.
💰 Role 4: Finance & Escrow Admin (Payment Coordinator)
teme-admin.html > Payments Tab & Telegram Escrow Group
🎯 Daily Goal: Collect company commission and ensure tutors are paid securely.
💎 Commission Model:
• First Month: Teme Astegni collects 30% commission of the first month's tutoring fee from the parent or tutor.
• Tutor Platform Fee: 200 ETB flat registration/upgrade fee.
☑️ Daily Checklist:
  • Monitor incoming bank slips in the Payment Receipts Telegram Group (CBE / Telebirr).
  • Match the transaction reference number with the Parent ID (AST-P-XXXX) and Tutor ID (AST-T-XXXX).
  • Mark payment as "Verified / Active" in teme-admin.html.
  • Release the confirmed tutoring schedule and exchange contact details between the tutor and the parent.
