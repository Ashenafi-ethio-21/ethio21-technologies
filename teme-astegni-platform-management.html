STAFF PLAYBOOKS & CHECKLISTS

👥 Admin Team Roles & Daily SOPs

Select a role below to view their specific daily workflow, tools, and interactive checklist.

👑 Founder / Super Admin (Owner)

Responsible for overall platform governance, staff delegation, escrow balance, and system configuration.

Primary Tool: teme-admin.html (Owner Mode)

📋 Daily Checklist (Interactive)

  • Check daily KPI metrics (New Tutors, Active Matches, Weekly Revenue).
  • Review Google Sheets CRM backup to ensure webhook sync is active.
  • Audit Admin accounts & assign Coordinator duties in "Admins" tab.
  • Review flagged disputes or parent satisfaction ratings.

⚡ Key Rules & Protocols

  • Master Passkey: Never share Owner passkeys; create individual Coordinator logins.
  • Commission Target: Ensure 30% first-month cut is preserved on all confirmed contracts.
  • Tutor Upgrades: Manually authorize Tier Level 3 & 4 Master Tutors.

📋 KYC & Admissions Coordinator

The guardian of trust: verifies tutor identity, National Fayda ID, and university academic credentials.

Primary Tool: teme-admin.html > Tutors Tab

📋 Verification Checklist

  • Open "Tutors" tab and filter by "Pending Verification".
  • Verify National Fayda ID (16-digit FIN) or Kebele ID authenticity.
  • Verify University ID card: Tutor must be 2nd Year+ (No 1st-year freshmen).
  • Inspect portrait photo: ensure clear, professional appearance.
  • Click "✅ Verify Tutor" to grant live badge and enable matching.

🚫 Rejection & Safety Criteria

  • Fraud Detection: Automatic instant reject if university ID name does not match Fayda ID.
  • Document Blur: Reject if student ID expiration date or faculty is unreadable.
  • Underage Rule: University tutors must be at least 18 years old.

🤝 Matchmaker & Operations Coordinator

Pairs verified tutors with families based on geographical proximity, subject mastery, and schedule compatibility.

Primary Tool: teme-admin.html > Matches Tab

📋 Matchmaking Checklist

  • Review incoming parent lead: check Grade, Location, and Days/Week.
  • Call parent within 2 hours to confirm specific tutoring goals.
  • Filter Tutors list by same sub-city/neighborhood (Bole, CMC, Meganagna, etc.).
  • Ensure tutor current student load is < 3 (Strict 3/3 capacity limit).
  • Click "🔗 Create Match" to link contracts and notify both parties.

📍 Spatial & Capacity Rules

  • Transit Rule: Never assign tutors living > 8 km from the student's home to prevent tardiness.
  • Subject Match: High school grades (9-12) strictly require Natural Science or Engineering tutors for Math/Physics.
  • Gender Preference: Strictly honor parent requests for female tutors if specified.

💰 Finance & Escrow Admin

Validates parent bank payment slips, enforces the 30% first-month commission, and manages tutor payout releases.

Primary Tool: Telegram Escrow Hub & Admin Payments

📋 Payment Verification Checklist

  • Monitor Payment Receipts Telegram Stream for new Telebirr / CBE slips.
  • Cross-check transaction reference number with bank app to prevent forged slips.
  • Deduct Teme Astegni 30% first-month commission into company reserve.
  • Mark match as "Payment Approved" in Admin Portal.
  • Release authorized schedule & contact card to both tutor and family.

💳 Commission & Fee Structure

  • First Month Tutoring: 30% platform commission retained by Teme Astegni.
  • Months 2+: Direct tutor payment or platform recurring escrow (optional guarantee).
  • Tutor Platform Fee: Flat 200 ETB for badge activation and portal upgrades.
