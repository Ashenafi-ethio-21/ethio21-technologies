Month-to-Month Contract Management (Continuations vs. Cancellations)

How active ongoing tutoring contracts are handled in Month 2, Month 3, and beyond without losing history or consuming database space:

🟢 Case A: Contract RENEWS (Continues Next Month)

When a parent and tutor decide to continue tutoring for another month:

  1. Stays Active in Firestore: The match document in astegni_matches remains active. Active contracts are tiny (300 bytes of text) and take negligible space.
  2. Record Payment: The coordinator records the renewal payment in the Admin Portal.
  3. Sync to Sheet: Tap ☁️ Sync Confirmed Matches to Sheet. Google Sheet records the new month's commission row in Matches_Ledger.
  4. Classes Continue: Lessons proceed smoothly with zero interruption.

🔴 Case B: Contract STOPS (Cancellation / Completion)

When tutoring ends (e.g., student passed national exam or relocated):

  1. Mark Status Closed: Admin switches the match status dropdown to 🔒 Closed (የተጠናቀቀ).
  2. Update Ledger: The final closure status is pushed to Google Sheets.
  3. Prune from Hot Desk: Because the contract is now permanently finished and archived in Google Sheets and Telegram, it can be deleted from Firestore to free space.
  4. Tutor Slot Frees Up: The tutor automatically gets an open slot to accept a new family!

💡 What if an old parent calls back 6 months later?

Their historical records are permanently preserved in your Telegram Parents Group (`-1003632707084`) and in your Google Sheet. Simply search their phone number or tag (e.g. #AST_P_1234) to see all past tutors and feedback, then create a new match in seconds!
